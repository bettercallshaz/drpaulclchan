About Dr Paul

A word on how I work

Real change rarely happens on the surface. We'll go as deep as you're ready to go.

My approach uses Neuro-Linguistic Programming, work on values and beliefs, and, when it truly helps, hypnotherapy techniques that I'm certified in. None of this is about tricks or quick fixes. It's about understanding how your mind already works, so the changes we make together really last.

Anything we explore together, including any technique such as hypnotherapy, is always explained beforehand and used only with your full understanding and consent. This is your process, at your pace.
